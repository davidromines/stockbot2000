Where the project stands

Eleven of twelve build phases are complete, and on 24 September Phase 13 (the research engine), Addendum A (the five-slot trading engine) and Addendum C (the survivorship data) were built. You armed LIVE at 15:39 UTC on 24 September: the slot trader now places real orders in the Agentic account every five minutes in market hours. Addendum C (revision 2) — Survivorship-bias-free universe reconstruction
Built · in the ranking

Rebuilds which US companies existed (Layer A) and generates tagged, cohort-conditioned synthetic price histories for the ~7,000 delisted companies with no free prices (Layer B). Real rows are never touched; every synthetic row carries its source, cohort, method, inferred reason and seed, and a loader runs any strategy five ways: as generated, excluding synthetic rows, delistings at −100%, at 0%, real rows only. Synthetic data here corrects survivorship bias; it is not a substitute for real data.

100%

Feasibility, stated plainly. Free data supports 1996–2024: SEC electronic filing phased in 1993–96 (the 1993 Q1 index is 1 KB, 1996 Q1 is 7.8 MB), so an earlier universe would be mostly imputed. The small failing companies that matter most have the least real data to calibrate from. The existing generator is distinguishable from real data at AUC 0.978, so synthetic rows stay out of discovery and promotion until a new one passes that test. Sources verified accessible today: SEC EDGAR, FinanceDatabase (delisted flag and sector, no dates), plus the Alpha Vantage, Internet Archive and 8-K data already loaded.

Addendum A — decisions for your review

Each has the default the build will follow unless you change it. Decision 2 is answered.

1
Rank slots on gross or net P&L?
§7 prioritises gross; §6 says a strategy that loses money isn't successful. A strategy can be positive gross and negative net, and then it loses money in the account. Default: Rank on net using measured execution costs where live fills exist, modeled costs otherwise; gross, costs and net shown side by side.
2
Is the Agentic account margin or cash?
Answered: limited margin (Robinhood reports limited_margin, not cash). Both the pattern-day-trader limit and the settled-funds rule are enforced by the I12 guard.
3
How much evidence before a strategy gets a slot? — answered: none; the ranking decides
The league's current rank threshold is 60 sessions, which would leave every slot in cash for about three months. Default: A separate, configurable slot threshold — proposed 20 forward sessions, 10 closed trades, positive net P&L, drawdown under a set limit. The 60-session rank stays as the "established" tier.
4
What role do backtests play? — answered: the starting score
The null and the SPY benchmark stop being promotion requirements (§5–6), and §29 says never select on backtest alone. Default: A positive net backtest admits a strategy to paper trading; only forward (paper or live) evidence wins a slot. Null and benchmark are reported, not gating.
5
Intraday data source
Continuous stops need live quotes; the database holds daily bars, and free intraday history covers about 60 days. Default: Poll quotes for held positions and candidates at a configurable interval; intraday strategies are proven forward on paper until an intraday history source exists.
6
Current real positions
The Agentic account holds positions placed by hand on 09-14. Default: Adopt them as legacy positions, exit each under its recorded stop, and open nothing new outside the slot engine.
7
Arming live execution — armed by you, 09-24 15:39 UTC
§24 reserves account authorization for a human. Default: I14 ends with LIVE built, tested on SIMULATION and SHADOW, and ready to arm; arming it is your step.
