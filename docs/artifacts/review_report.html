3Architecture

There are three layers, and the code keeps them from calling each other. The research factory creates and backtests ideas. The league and ranking paper-trade and score them. Capital deployment trades only through the risk and execution layer. The research code cannot import the broker; a regression test enforces this.

Design rules that bear load

  • Fail closed on unknowns. An unknown market cap, liquidity or correlation blocks a trade; it is never treated as average.
  • Append-only records. Strategy identity and version (league.py contains no UPDATE), decisions, the trial counter and accounting. Restatements sit beside originals with an accounting version.
  • Risk limits live apart from strategy config. config/risk.yaml is separate so that a strategy edit cannot widen a limit. LIVE is armed by one line there, and changing it is the owner's step.
  • Idempotency. signal_id is a content hash; broker orders carry a ref_id; after a restart the ledger is rebuilt from the orders table. The new options path (R7) reuses the identical pattern rather than inventing a second one.
  • Broker is authoritative. Every LIVE run reconciles the slot log against Robinhood positions and halts on any difference.
  • A slot's P&L is scoped to its current occupant. Slots get reassigned to different strategies over time; a trade placed under the previous occupant must never be counted against the new one. Pinned by a regression test added this period (test_daily_snapshot.py).

Integrity rules (unchanged since last review)

  • Point in time. Fundamentals become visible only from the first session after SEC acceptance plus a 2-day lag. Market cap = shares from the filing × price on the filing date.
  • Freshness. The newest completed session is read from a liquid ETF at the source, never from the table being updated.
  • Absence is not zero. Missing debt, R&D or EPS stays missing, and any ratio built from it is missing too.
  • Backups. Forward and decision tables are dumped nightly to backups/forward.sql.gz, committed to git.

7Strategies and the ranking — what's new this period

Two stages finished building

Stage O, the Knowledge Strategy Factory, completed O1–O13 on 2026-10-05. Documented trading knowledge (academic papers, published factors, open-source strategy collections) now reproduces, varies within declared bounds, and is queued as seeds for governed machine mutation — inert until the evolutionary search is unfrozen, and still never importing the frozen search module directly (a regression test enforces the boundary). 153 JKP published factors are now seeded as KNOWN_FACTOR entries.

Stage R, options trading, completed R1–R7 on 2026-10-12. options_live.py mirrors a proven option strategy's newest paper trade for real: whole contracts, one leg at a time, the live ask, the same review-then-place-with-a-fixed-ref_id idempotency as the stock LIVE path. A regression bug was found and fixed while verifying it (the test's fake option price exceeded its own trade budget, so its "proven" scenario silently never fired). Built does not mean armed: allow_options is off, the account has no option level, and the best options paper record today is 3 closed trades against a 20-trade bar — so this is correctly inert, not merely unarmed.

The headline finding: seven families clear t > 2 for the first time

See §2 for the table. This comes from the same diagnostic funnel (Stage T) that has run nightly since 2026-09-27 and, until now, consistently reported that no family had enough forward evidence to say anything. The two mechanical reasons the sample grew large enough: (1) nearly a thousand Lab validation survivors that had never been paper-traded were all enrolled in late September, and (2) three weeks of additional calendar time have passed. Neither reason implies the signal is real — they explain why a t-stat exists to compute at all.

Ranking formula (unchanged)

score = (k × backtest + n × paper) / (k + n), k = 10 prior trades, n = closed paper trades, both net return per trade. A strategy whose dead-company backtest loses money is out. Slots take the top five tradeable strategies, at most four from one family.

14Questions for the reviewer

  1. Seven families clear t > 2 — what would make this believable rather than artifact #14? A correlation/effective-n check on lab_search (many of its 5,560 trades share a ticker and date with another fund); a fresh holdout look now that the sample exists; or is more calendar time the only honest answer?
  2. Is "20 closed paper trades, net positive" the right bar for R7 (real-money options)? It is built and gated on exactly that bar; today's best is 3.
  3. Should Lab-discovered strategies be required to produce a readable description before they can hold a slot, given 3 of 5 slots currently cannot be explained to a person without reading their raw rule text?
  4. Evidence floor (standing question). Should real capital wait for a minimum forward record before a new assignment, or is "top five of a blended score" still defensible at $100 scale, now that a forward record exists to check the backtest against?
  5. Order of the audit. The restatement and the funnel corrections (G1–G3) are done. Is the proposed order right from here: A4–A6 (multiple testing, the September enrollment, a per-family verdict), then the eligibility gate and ranking work, now that the drawdown tiers are merged?
  6. Synthetic dead companies (standing question). Is a tuned synthetic reconstruction acceptable for choosing strategies, or should the ranking gate on the worst case until real delisted prices are bought?
  7. Horizon comparability (standing question). How should strategies with 5-day, 60-day and option holds be put on one scale?
