## Needs the owner's decision

1. **Nothing reports an outage.** The VM was down 5½ days and nobody was told: the Telegram alerts run on the VM
   itself. Fix: a free external dead-man check (e.g. healthchecks.io) that cron pings every 5 minutes and that messages
   the owner when pings stop. Needs an account in the owner's name.
2. **The sign-in alert is false.** Daily "Robinhood sign-in renewal due (≤0 days left)" assumes a 30-day login life;
   the login is 44 days old and still refreshes (it survived the 5½-day outage). Options: renew anyway (safe), and/or
   alert only when a refresh actually fails (which already halts trading and alerts).
3. **Obsolete messages from the manual-order era.** Every successful daily run (~1 AM PT) sends "today's orders" — the
   September manual slate (SELL TNON/VISN/ANTX, BUY SBET/CHRD/USNA) — and a fund report. The bot has traded on its own
   since 09-24. Recommend retiring: stage [6] daily book (it also runs `daily_picks.py` twice), `orders.py --build`,
   `notify.py --slate`, `fund_report.py --notify`; and `eod_report.py` at 21:10, which has written a file nobody reads
   since 10-21.
4. **The two defects from the 15% review** (live path, outside market hours, on the owner's go): no re-entry in a
   stopped name for N sessions (OVV, 11-05); reconciliation skips a symbol with an order in flight (RWM, 10-28).
5. **Overdue decisions:** the 15% review's options (B3 basket slots LIVE, B4 release on a negative ≥20-trade paper
   record, the pre-registered fallback) and the post-freeze decisions due ~11-16.
