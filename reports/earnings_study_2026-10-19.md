# Earnings-date study — 2026-10-19 (Stage AD3.5)

17,188 announcements (8-K 2.02, 2015-2025), liquid common stock. Window: open of the session before the announcement to the open 5 sessions after; control: the same stock, same length, ending 10 sessions earlier, no announcement inside. Excess over SPY.

| | across the announcement | control | difference (t) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| mean excess | -0.10% | +0.03% | -0.13% (-1.3) |
| sd of excess | 11.30% | 6.90% | x1.64 |
| share with |excess| > 10% | 25.8% | 8.6% | |

**Reading (rule stated before running): holding through earnings adds risk without a measurable reward — a skip rule is worth backtesting for non-earnings strategies.**

Caveats: survivors only (the database's own bias); 8-K 2.02 marks the release, which can precede the call; a one-sided read on the mean; this is a population average, not any one strategy's trades.
